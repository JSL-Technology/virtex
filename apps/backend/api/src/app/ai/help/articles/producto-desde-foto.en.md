## How to do it

Under Inventory › Products › New, upload a photo of the product or scan its barcode with the camera. The name, description, brand, barcode, category, type, unit and tax are proposed, in your company's books language. Suggested fields carry their confidence, and only the fields you have not touched are filled.

## Duplicates

Before creating, you are warned if a product already exists with the same barcode, a very similar name and, if image search is on, an almost identical photo.

## Price and cost

Price and cost are not guessed from the photo: the usual margin of similar products is shown as a reference, but you set the values.
