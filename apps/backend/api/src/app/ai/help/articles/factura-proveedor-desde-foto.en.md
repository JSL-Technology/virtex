title: Entering a supplier bill from a photo or a PDF

## How to do it

Under Purchasing › Supplier bills › New, press "Read photo or PDF" and choose the photo of the bill or the PDF the supplier sent you (up to 20 MB). The form is filled with what is read from the document: supplier, document number, dates, currency, lines, tax and, where the country uses them, the legal service charge, the tax authority's payment form and the purchase classification. Suggested fields carry a mark with their confidence.

## Read by your company's country

The document is read and checked by the rules of your company's country: the tax identifier printed there (RNC in the Dominican Republic, RFC in Mexico, EIN in the United States…), the number the document carries (NCF, CFDI folio fiscal, NF-e key, CUFE or simply the supplier's invoice number), how dates are written and which tax applies. If your company's country has no fiscal rules configured, the identifier and the number are copied as printed and the form warns that they were not checked.

## What is checked

Before showing you anything, the system checks the supplier's tax identifier by its country's rule, the shape of the document number (where the country has one that can be checked), that the lines add up to the subtotal and that subtotal plus taxes gives the total. If something does not add up, a warning appears and the doubtful value is not written into the field. It also warns if you already entered a bill from that supplier with the same number, or if the same file is already the document of another bill.

## The supplier

The supplier is found by its tax identifier, never by name. If you have none with that identifier and the country's authority can be consulted, the warning shows the registered name and a "Register supplier" link that opens the record already filled in.

## The lines

Each line is matched to what you did before with that supplier: if last time you booked it to an expense account or a product, the same is proposed. Otherwise the product is looked up by its code or its name. Check each line's account before saving.

## Nothing is saved on its own

What is read is a suggestion: it fills only the fields you have not touched, and nothing is recorded until you press Save. On saving, the file is attached to the bill as its evidence; you can open it from the bill itself.

## Without AI

If your plan does not include reading documents, the button is called "Attach photo or PDF": the file is kept with the bill, but you fill in the fields.
