## What it is

You can connect an AI assistant you already use, such as Claude or ChatGPT, to Virtex through the MCP protocol. The assistant looks up your data and prepares drafts without copying and pasting. In Settings › Integrations, the "Connected AI agents" section shows the address to give the assistant.

## What it can do

When you connect it, Virtex asks for your permission on its own screen: which assistant it is, where you will return afterwards and what it will be able to do. It acts as you, in the company you are in, with your same permissions and branches; if you lose a permission tomorrow, the assistant loses it too. It can look up the information you have access to and, if you allow it, propose drafts (invoices, quotes, purchase orders, payment reminders). A draft is never created from the assistant: it gives you a link to Virtex, where you see the proposal and decide whether to confirm or discard it.

## Requirements

You need the AI assistant permission, the plan must include the assistant, and the company must not have turned it off in its AI settings.

## Security

Every lookup by the assistant is recorded in the access log under its name. Access lasts one hour and renews itself only while you stay connected; "Disconnect" cuts it off immediately.
