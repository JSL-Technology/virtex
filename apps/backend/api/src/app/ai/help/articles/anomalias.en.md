## What it is

Analysis › Anomalies gathers what the system found unusual in sales, purchases, inventory, cash and security: duplicated supplier bills, sales below cost, unusual discounts, large inventory adjustments, duplicated payments, purchases billed that do not match what was ordered or received, price changes, changes after issuing and off-hours activity, among others.

## How to work with it

Each finding shows its evidence with its figures. You can ask for a plain-language explanation (it spends one AI credit). Mark each one as useful or as a false positive: that decision teaches the system which detectors matter to your company. A new serious finding also reaches you as a notice in the bell.

## Important

An anomaly is a lead for a person to review, not an accusation. Many findings have a legitimate explanation.
