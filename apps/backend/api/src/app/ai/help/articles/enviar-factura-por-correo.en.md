## How it is sent

In the detail of an issued invoice, use "Send by e-mail". If you leave the address blank, the customer's is used. The e-mail carries the invoice PDF and goes out under your company's name; the customer's replies reach the company's contact address. A draft cannot be sent: it has to be issued first.

## A message drafted with AI

You can write a message for the customer or have it drafted: choose the tone (cordial, formal or firm) and press "Draft with AI". The message is written in the customer's language and mentions only figures from the invoice itself — number, total and due date; if the invoice is overdue, it says so clearly. It appears in the box for you to read and change; nothing is sent until you press Send. It needs the assistant in your plan and spends one AI credit.
