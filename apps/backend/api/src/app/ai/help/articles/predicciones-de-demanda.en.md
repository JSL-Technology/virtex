## What it shows

Analysis › Predictions shows, for each product and warehouse, how much is expected to sell in the coming weeks, with a range of what is likely, the days of stock you have left and whether you risk running out before an order arrives. When it is time to restock, it proposes a quantity to order.

## Where it comes from

The forecast is computed every night from the sales history, the country's holidays, prices and discounts, and your suppliers' real lead times (measured from your receipts). A product with little history has a wider range; the screen shows the forecast quality of each one.

## Notices

When a new stockout risk appears, you get a notice in the bell. From the assistant you can ask "what is about to run out?" or have it prepare a draft purchase order with the suggested quantities.
