## What it is

Analysis › Manager's report sums up the month in a few paragraphs for whoever runs the company: how sales, margin and result went against the previous month; where cash stands and where it is heading; who owes the company and whom it owes; what to do about inventory; the anomalies worth a look, and the recommended actions for the next month.

## When it is written

It is written by itself on the 1st of each month for the month that ended, and announced in the bell to those who may read it. You can also ask for any closed month by choosing the month and pressing "Write the month's report".

## Where the figures come from

Every figure comes from the financial statements, the receivables and payables ageing, the cash position, the projected cash flow, collections, anomalies and the system's predictions. The assistant writes but does not calculate: if its text mentions a figure that is not in those data, the report is refused and written again. If a source was not available (for example, because your plan does not include that feature), the report says so.

A report a person asks for is written only from what that person can see (their permissions and branches) and only they read it. The one on the 1st covers the whole company and is read only by those who hold the manager's report permission and also the permission of every source it uses (for example, financial statements or receivables), and who are not limited to some branches.

## Printing or saving as PDF

"Print or save PDF" opens the browser's print dialog with the report alone, without menus; choose "Save as PDF" to get the file.
