---
title: The ERP assistant
---

## What it is

The assistant answers questions about your company with the ERP's own data: sales, receivables and payables, inventory, financial statements, cash, forecasts and anomalies. It opens with the sparkle button on the top bar or with Ctrl+J (⌘J on a Mac).

## What it can see

It sees exactly what your permissions let you see, nothing more. If your role has no access to the financial statements, neither does the assistant. If you are limited to some branches, it does not use company-wide figures drawn from every branch. Each answer lists the sources it consulted below it, so you know where every figure comes from.

## What it can do for you

It can prepare draft quotes, invoices, purchase orders and payment reminders. It never creates them on its own: it shows you a card with the preview, and the document is created, as a draft, when you press "Create draft". If the figures changed since the proposal (a price, a tax or a date), nothing is created and you are asked to look again. You then review and issue it from its own screen, like any other. The assistant does not move money, approve documents or change permissions.

## Conversations

Each conversation is kept and only you see it. You can go back to an earlier one from "Conversations" or start a new one. A proposal you do not confirm expires after 24 hours.

## Requirements

The assistant is part of the Professional and Enterprise plans, and your role needs the "ERP assistant (AI)" permission. Each answer spends AI credits from your plan; usage is shown under Administration › AI.
