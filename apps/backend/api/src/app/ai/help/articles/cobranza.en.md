## What it shows

Sales › Collections lists every invoice with an open balance you can see (your branches'), in the order it pays to chase them. Each row gives the suggested action and why: a large balance, unlikely to be paid soon, more than 60 days overdue, a broken promise, a current promise or contacted recently.

## How it is ordered

The priority combines four things:

- the balance, in the company's currency;
- the chance the invoice is paid within 30 days without doing anything, taken from the projected cash flow (how that customer actually pays). If there is no projection yet, it is estimated from the invoice's age and the screen says so;
- the age: the more overdue, the more it weighs;
- what was already done: a broken promise raises it; a current promise or a contact in the last three days lowers it.

## The suggested action

- **Call**: there is a broken promise or the invoice is more than 60 days overdue.
- **Firm reminder**: 31 to 60 days overdue.
- **Reminder**: up to 30 days overdue.
- **Notice before it is due**: due this week and unlikely to be paid on time.
- **Wait for the promise** or **Contacted recently**: no need to insist for now.

## Sending a reminder

When you open an invoice you can write the message or ask the assistant to draft it in the suggested tone (cordial, formal or firm). The assistant uses only the invoice's figures; if its text mentions any other figure, it is refused. Review the message and press "Send reminder": it goes by e-mail under your company's name, in the invoice's language and with the invoice attached. You can also keep it as a draft.

The ERP assistant can leave a reminder as a draft ("draft a reminder to Late Payer LLC"); it never sends it: the draft waits in Collections for someone to review it.

## Logging calls and promises

Log each call (answered, no answer, promised to pay, disputes the invoice…), payment promises with their date and, if agreed, the amount, and any note. A promise whose date passes with the invoice still open counts as broken from the next day.

## Automating

Workflows include the automatic "Payment reminder" step: it leaves the reminder as a draft for review or sends it directly. It is usually placed behind an approval.
