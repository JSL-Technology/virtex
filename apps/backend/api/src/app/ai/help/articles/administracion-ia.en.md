title: AI administration, credits and usage
---

## Where to find it

In Settings (the gear in the user menu, or Ctrl+,), the "Artificial intelligence" section shows which AI features your plan includes and whether they are available right now, the credits used this month and their limit, usage and cost by feature, and how the latest forecasting and anomaly runs went.

## Credits

Every use of a model spends AI credits from your plan: an assistant answer, reading a supplier bill, drafting a message, explaining an anomaly or creating a product from a photo. The nightly forecast and anomaly detection do not spend credits this way. When the month's credits run out, the AI features stop answering until the next period or until the plan is upgraded; the rest of the ERP keeps working exactly the same.

## What the company allows

On the same screen, "What the company allows" decides what goes to the AI provider: switching language models off entirely (forecasts and anomalies are computed inside the platform and keep working), switching features off one by one, whether the manager's report is written by itself on the 1st (off by default, since it sends the financial statements without anyone asking), whether product photos are sent to find duplicates by image (off by default), and hiding tax identifiers, e-mails and bank accounts from the provider when the answer does not need them. Changing it needs the "AI settings" permission and every change is audited.

## Budget

Besides the plan's credits, the company has a monthly budget in dollars (and, if it wants, a daily one). You are notified at 50, 80 and 100%; at 100% the AI features stop until the next period or until it is raised, and the rest of the ERP keeps working.

## Conversations and your data

Assistant conversations are deleted overnight once they pass the configured number of days without activity, and so are those of anyone who leaves the company. What the assistant proposed and what was decided is kept as a record. Each person can download or delete their own conversations from the assistant.
